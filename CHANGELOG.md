## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-09

### Added

- Sidebar footer force-restart button with confirmation and busy state.
- Host route at `POST /dsh-restart-plugin/force-restart`.
- Detached PowerShell restart helper launched through WMI, with automatic cleanup.
- Chinese and English documentation.
- MIT license, security policy, and GitHub CI checks.
